    Where your things go
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Drop
    
        
    Text and files are saved on this server with the expiry you choose: 2, 4 or 7 days. Then they're deleted, and a cleanup job removes anything left behind.
    
        
    A password is stored only as a bcrypt hash. Without it, anyone holding the link can open what you shared.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Beam
    
        
    The server only introduces the two browsers. After that, text and files travel directly between them over WebRTC, encrypted end to end.
    
        
    When a direct path isn't possible, the data goes through a relay on this server. It stays encrypted there, and nothing is written to disk.
